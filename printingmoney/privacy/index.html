Privacy

This page explains what information this website collects when you request or buy Printing Money, how we use it, and who helps us provide the service.

What we collect

  • Purchase request form: your full name and email address and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number, business or organization, and message, plus your acknowledgment of how the request works.
  • Payment: Hakeem emails you a payment link. The payment provider in that link collects your payment details on its own secure page. We receive your name, email address and payment status — never your full card number.
  • Order records: your request, payment confirmation and delivery are kept in Jireh Strategies’ business email.

Spam and abuse prevention

The request form uses a hidden anti-spam field and a quick “I’m not a robot” check (Google reCAPTCHA) on FormSubmit’s page before your request is sent.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, send you a payment link, process and fulfill your order, deliver your ebook, provide support, and keep sales records.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists or send you marketing email.
  • This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

Service providers

Each of these providers processes information only to provide its service:

  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • FormSubmit (delivers your request to Hakeem’s email and sends your confirmation email)
  • Google reCAPTCHA (spam check before a request is sent)
  • Microsoft Outlook (business email)
  • The payment provider named in your payment link (payment processing)

Contact

Questions about your information? Email jirehstrategies@outlook.com.
